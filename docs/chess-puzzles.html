Free chess puzzles from real games, right here in your browser. No account: your rating goes up when you solve and down when you miss, and the next puzzle matches it.

How it works

The opponent plays first; then it is your turn. Find the best move, and keep going until the combination is over. A wrong move does not end the puzzle: try again, but it already counts as missed. Any checkmate counts, even one that is not the stored answer.

What you will train

Forks, pins, skewers, discovered attacks, mates in one and in two, sacrifices and endgame technique. Every puzzle comes from a real game, from beginner level to master, and when you solve it you see which theme it was.

Tactics decide most games below 2000: one missed fork or one undefended piece. Solving a few puzzles a day trains your eye to see them in your own games.

The puzzle of the day

Everyone gets the same puzzle each day, chosen from positions rated around 1000 to 1600. It does not change your rating: it is a short daily habit.

Questions

Are these chess puzzles free?
Yes, free and without an account. Your rating and the puzzles you have done are kept only in this browser.
How is my puzzle rating calculated?
Like a chess rating: every puzzle has its own rating, and you win or lose points against it. Solving a harder puzzle earns more. Using a hint leaves your rating as it is.
Where do the puzzles come from?
From the Lichess puzzle database, which is in the public domain (CC0): positions taken from real games and checked with an engine.
Are there more puzzles in the app?
Yes. Chess Coach has over 35,000, plus Streak and Storm modes, and a coach that explains your mistakes in your own games.
