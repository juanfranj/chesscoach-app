Why another chess app

Engines are free. Understanding isn't.

Any phone can run an engine stronger than any human alive. It shows you the move and a number. It never tells you what you walked into, and a number is not a lesson. Chess Coach keeps the engine and adds the part that was missing: a plain sentence about the idea you missed, written for your rating.

Practice Lab

A game has three parts. So does the training.

Not a pile of exercises. Three labs, one for each phase of the game, each with the same shape: learn the idea first, then play it out against an opponent that will punish you for getting it wrong. And a fourth for the skill all three depend on: calculation.

The four labs are Premium. Free to try: the Italian Game in the opening trainer, the first middlegame exercise, and the first ten positions of each calculation set.

Under the hood

One engine cannot do all three jobs

Judging a position, playing like a person and fitting inside an iPhone are different problems. They run on your device, not on a server, so analysis works on the underground with no signal.

Stockfish 18

The one that judges

On Android it runs on the phone as its own process, with its neural evaluation. It is what marks your blunders, draws the evaluation graph and finds the move you missed, and it is what the strongest rivals play with.

Caissa

The one on iPhone and iPad

Not a whim. Stockfish is GPLv3 and iOS does not let an app start a separate process, so linking it inside the app would put the whole app under that licence. Caissa does the same job under a licence that lets it live in there, and it analyses and plays on the device just the same.

Maia

The one that plays like a person

Not a strong engine turned down. Each level is a different network trained on real games by players of that strength, so it goes wrong where people go wrong and plays the move a club player of that rating would actually pick. It is the rival in the Opening Lab.

One more, for honesty: below about 1300 the official Stockfish cannot be made weaker — its weakest setting still plays at roughly 1320. The beginner rivals run on Fairy-Stockfish instead, which can, so a bot labelled 800 loses the way an 800 loses rather than by throwing a piece at random.

How the coach is powered

Bring your own key, or don't think about it

The engine runs on your phone. The explanations come from a language model, and you choose how you pay for that: credits inside the app, or your own API key with one of five providers, billed by them at cost.

Credits

Buy a pack and forget about it. Free calls to start with, so you can see what the coach sounds like before paying anything.

Your key

Paste an API key and the app talks to that provider directly. Typical cost of a full game review is a fraction of a cent.

Privacy

Keys are encrypted on the device and never leave it except to reach the provider you chose. No ads, no trackers, no selling anybody's games.
