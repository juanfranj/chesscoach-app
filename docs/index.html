Under the hood

One engine can’t do all three jobs

Judging a position, playing like a person and fitting inside an iPhone are different problems. They all run on your device, not on a server, so analysis works with no signal.

The one that judges

Stockfish 18

On Android it runs on the phone with its neural evaluation. It marks your blunders, draws the evaluation graph and finds the move you missed.

The one on iPhone and iPad

Caissa

iOS doesn’t let an app start a separate process, and Stockfish’s licence would cover the whole app if it lived inside. Caissa does the same job under a licence that lets it, on the device just the same.

The one that plays like a person

Maia

Not a strong engine turned down: a network trained on real games by players of each strength, so it plays the move a club player would actually pick. It is the rival in the Opening Lab.

Below about 1300 the official Stockfish can’t be made weaker, so the beginner rivals run on Fairy-Stockfish, which can: a bot labelled 800 loses the way an 800 loses, not by throwing a piece at random. The engines are GPL and their sources are published: open source notice.
